Accuracy and timing model

Global shadow geometry uses Shadowline’s WGS 84 ellipsoid. Local contacts and the disc view use Astronomy Engine’s topocentric observer height with an operational ΔT of 69.1734 seconds for 12 August 2026, derived from the IERS finals2000A Earth-orientation prediction.

The solar-disc preview is topocentric. When GPS provides no altitude, online map and coordinate selections opportunistically use Mapterhorn’s Copernicus GLO-30 terrain elevation, then retain it with the location. Predictions use a smooth lunar limb, so mountains and valleys on the Moon can still move the observed onset of beads and totality by seconds. Terrain blocking the local horizon is not modelled.

The device clock is used until the CloudFront edge clock can be sampled.
