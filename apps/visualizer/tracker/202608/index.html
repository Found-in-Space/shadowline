What this prediction can—and can’t—show

These times and views are predictions, not direct observations. They are calculated for the location and height shown above, using the shape of Earth and how it is expected to turn on eclipse day.

If GPS cannot find your height, we may estimate it from a terrain map while you are online. The model gives the Moon a smooth edge. Its real mountains and valleys can shift the first and last flashes of sunlight by a few seconds. It also cannot see nearby hills, buildings or clouds that may block your view.

Use GPS or a checked location for the final times. Treat the prediction as a guide, be ready at least a minute early, and keep watching.

The countdown is using this device’s clock. When online, it will check the time and correct it if needed.
