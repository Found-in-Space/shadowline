How accurate are these times?

The times are calculated for the latitude, longitude and height shown above. The calculation includes the shape of Earth and its predicted rotation on 12 August 2026.

If GPS does not give us your height, we may estimate it from a terrain map while you are online. The calculation treats the edge of the Moon as smooth, but real mountains and valleys on the Moon can change the first and last flashes of sunlight by a few seconds. Nearby hills and buildings that block your view are not included.

Use GPS or checked coordinates for the final times, and be ready at least a minute early.

The countdown is using this device’s clock until it can check the time online.
