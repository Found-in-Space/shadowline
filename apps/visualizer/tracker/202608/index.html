About these eclipse predictions

Times and views are predicted for the location and elevation shown above.

The Moon’s mountains and valleys can shift the first and last flashes of sunlight by a few seconds. Nearby hills, buildings and clouds may also block your view.

Use GPS or a location you have checked, be ready at least a minute early and keep watching.
